Research

Research Paper · DE10130 – Computer Networks
DDoS Attacks and Defense

An extensive analysis of Distributed Denial of Service attacks and defense mechanisms. Covers the history of DDoS attacks from the 1996 Panix incident to the 2013 Spamhaus attack, classifies the three main attack types (volumetric, protocol-based, and application layer), examines detection methods (signature-based and anomaly-based), and evaluates defense strategies including traffic filtering, firewalls, CDNs with Anycast routing, and anti-DDoS HTTP throttling.
